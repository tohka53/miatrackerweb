1 Delete your account from the app

This is the fastest route: deletion happens immediately, with no involvement from our team.

  1. 1 Open {{ brand }} on your device and sign in with the account you want to delete.
  2. 2 Tap the menu icon (☰) in the top-left corner to open the side panel.
  3. 3 Scroll to the bottom of the panel. Below Sign out you will find the red Delete account button.
  4. 4 Read the confirmation notice and tap Delete my account.
  5. 5 Your account and its data are erased at that moment and you are signed out automatically.

This action is permanent and cannot be undone. There is no grace period and no way to recover the information once deletion is confirmed. If you need to keep your inventory, export it first from the Reports section.

2 If you cannot access the app

For example, if you lost access to your email, uninstalled the app, or cannot sign in.

  1. 1 Write to {{ contactEmail }} from the email address you registered with.
  2. 2 Use “Account deletion request” as the subject and include the email address of the account you want deleted.
  3. 3 We verify your identity to make sure the request comes from the account holder.
  4. 4 We process the deletion within {{ emailSlaDays }} days at the latest and email you a confirmation when it is complete.

Shared company accounts

If your account belongs to an organization with other users, records documenting operations carried out jointly may be retained under that organization's account, stripped of the data that identifies you personally. Your profile, your credentials and your personal data are deleted all the same.

5 Deleting part of your data without deleting your account

You do not have to delete your account to remove information from it. While signed in you can delete individual inventory items, assets, locations, maintenance records and uploaded photos directly from their detail screen; each deletion removes the record and its images from our systems.

If you want a broader deletion — for example, every photo you uploaded, or all records from a given period — while keeping your account active, email {{ contactEmail }} describing what you want removed. The same {{ emailSlaDays }}-day window and the same retention periods described above apply.

Questions before you delete?

If you have questions about what happens to your information, write to {{ contactEmail }} before confirming the deletion. You can also read our Privacy Policy.
